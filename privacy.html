English

"Katerina Content Assistant" is a personal app used by a single person, the owner of the accounts katerina_opora and katerina.opora. It publishes the owner's own content after the owner's approval.

The app does not collect, store or share data about other users of Pinterest, Threads or any other service. The owner's access tokens are kept on a private server and are never published.
